本隱私權政策適用於 Google Play 上架之「HoloDash 電車儀表板」（套件名稱 tool.holodash，以下稱「本應用程式」），由自由數位科技有限公司（以下稱「本公司」或「我們」）開發與營運。把手機變成電動車儀表板的工具，可經藍牙讀取使用者自己車輛（特斯拉）的車況，並提供測速照相語音提醒。

當您下載、安裝或使用本應用程式，即表示您已閱讀並同意本政策及《使用者條款》。若您不同意，請停止使用並解除安裝。本政策依中華民國《個人資料保護法》（以下稱「個資法」）並參酌 Google Play 開發人員政策訂定。

2. 我們處理的資料

本應用程式不要求您提供姓名、身分證字號、電話、地址等可直接識別您的資料（另有說明者除外）。為提供功能，會處理下列資料：

位置資訊僅在您開啟「行車模式」時，以前景服務使用精確位置（GPS）計算車速、行進方向與前方測速照相距離；切到其他 APP 或關閉螢幕時，行車模式會以常駐通知繼續運作，您可隨時結束。位置只在裝置本機計算，不會上傳或分享。
行程與自訂提醒點行程紀錄（時間、里程、速度統計）與您新增的提醒點只儲存在您的裝置。
藍牙與車輛資料您在「車輛」分頁輸入的車輛識別號碼（VIN）與本應用程式產生的配對金鑰只儲存在您的裝置（私鑰以 Android Keystore 加密）。透過藍牙直接自您的車輛讀取車速、檔位、電量、胎壓、溫度、車上導航與播放中音樂等車況，僅用於即時顯示，不會上傳至任何伺服器。本應用程式不會對車輛送出開門、駕駛等控制指令（喚醒除外）。
購買紀錄訂閱或購買 Pro 時，由 Google Play 處理付款；本應用程式只向 Google Play 查詢購買狀態以啟用功能，不會取得您的付款資訊。
裝置與當機資訊可能透過 Google 服務取得非個人識別資訊（裝置型號、系統版本、當機紀錄），僅用於改善穩定性。

1. Scope and provider

This Privacy Policy applies to “HoloDash - EV Dashboard” on Google Play (package tool.holodash, the “App”), developed and operated by Liberty Digital Technology Co., Ltd. (“we”, “us”). Turns your phone into an EV dashboard. It can read your own vehicle's (Tesla) status over Bluetooth and provides speed camera voice alerts.

By downloading, installing or using the App, you confirm that you have read and agree to this Policy and our Terms of Use. If you do not agree, stop using and uninstall the App. This Policy follows Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act (“PDPA”) and the Google Play Developer Program Policies.

2. Data we process

The App does not ask for your name, national ID, phone number, address or other data that directly identifies you (unless stated otherwise). To provide its features, it processes the following:

LocationOnly while you turn on Drive mode, the App uses precise location (GPS) through a foreground service to calculate speed, heading and the distance to speed cameras ahead. When you switch apps or turn off the screen, Drive mode keeps running with an ongoing notification that you can stop at any time. Location is processed on your device only and is never uploaded or shared.
Trips & custom alertsTrip history (time, distance, speed statistics) and the alert points you add are stored only on your device.
Bluetooth & vehicle dataThe vehicle identification number (VIN) you enter and the pairing key generated by the App are stored only on your device (the private key is encrypted with Android Keystore). Vehicle status such as speed, gear, battery, tire pressure, temperatures, in-car navigation and now-playing media is read directly from your vehicle over Bluetooth only for live display and is never uploaded to any server. The App does not send unlock or driving commands to your vehicle (except a wake-up request).
PurchasesPayments for Pro are processed by Google Play. The App only queries Google Play for purchase status to unlock features and never receives your payment details.
Device & crash dataNon-identifying information (device model, OS version, crash logs) may be processed through Google services solely to improve stability.
