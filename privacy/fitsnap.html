1. 適用範圍與提供者

本隱私權政策適用於 Google Play 與 Apple App Store 上架之「FitSnap: AI Calorie & Gym Log」（中文名稱「FitSnap 健身熱量」，套件名稱／Bundle ID tool.fitsnap，以下稱「本應用程式」），由自由數位科技有限公司（以下稱「本公司」或「我們」）開發與營運。本應用程式讓您拍攝餐點照片，由 AI 估計熱量與營養素，並記錄重訓、有氧與體重。本應用程式適用 18 歲以上使用者，不是醫療器材，也不提供醫療或營養建議。

當您下載、安裝或使用本應用程式，即表示您已閱讀並同意本政策及《使用者條款》。若您不同意，請停止使用並解除安裝。本政策依中華民國《個人資料保護法》（以下稱「個資法」）並參酌 Google Play 開發人員政策與 Apple App Store 審查及隱私相關規範訂定。

2. 我們處理的資料

本應用程式免註冊，不要求您提供姓名、電子郵件、電話、地址等可直接識別您的資料。為提供功能，會處理下列資料：

餐點照片只有在您按下「分析這張照片」時，照片才會在手機上壓縮（最長邊 768 像素），經加密連線（HTTPS）傳送到本公司伺服器，再轉送至 OpenAI 進行辨識。本公司伺服器不保存照片，分析完成即丟棄；手機上的暫存照片也會刪除。您可另外輸入的文字補充（例如「半碗飯」）會一併送出，用途相同。
健康與健身資料您輸入的性別、年齡、身高、體重、活動量、目標，以及飲食、熱量與營養素、運動、喝水與體重紀錄，只儲存在您的裝置，不會上傳到本公司伺服器。
隨機裝置代碼與 IP 位址本應用程式在安裝時產生一組隨機代碼（不是廣告 ID，也不是手機序號）。每次分析時會連同照片送出，本公司伺服器只以其雜湊值與 IP 位址的雜湊值計算「每日使用次數」，防止濫用；這些計數只保留約 2 天。
購買紀錄訂閱 Pro 時，Android 由 Google Play、iOS 由 Apple App Store（StoreKit 2）處理付款。為提供 Pro 的每日分析次數，本應用程式會把商店提供的購買憑證送到本公司伺服器驗證：Android 為 Google Play 的 purchase token，iOS 為 StoreKit 2 的交易憑證（JWS）。伺服器向 Google 或 Apple 確認訂閱有效；我們不會取得您的付款卡號或其他付款資訊。
裝置與當機資訊可能透過 Google 服務取得非個人識別資訊（裝置型號、系統版本、當機紀錄），僅用於改善穩定性。

(“we”, “us”). The App lets you photograph a meal so AI can estimate its calories and macros, and lets you log strength training, cardio and body weight. The App is for adults 18+. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical or nutrition advice.

By downloading, installing or using the App, you confirm that you have read and agree to this Policy and our Terms of Use. If you do not agree, stop using and uninstall the App. This Policy follows Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act (“PDPA”), the Google Play Developer Program Policies, and Apple App Store Review and privacy requirements.

2. Data we process

The App needs no sign-up and never asks for your name, email, phone number or address. To provide its features, it processes the following:

Meal photosOnly when you tap “Analyze this photo”, the photo is compressed on your phone (longest side 768 px), sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our server and forwarded to OpenAI for recognition. Our server does not store photos; each one is discarded after analysis, and the temporary copy on your phone is deleted. Any optional note you type (for example “half portion”) is sent with it for the same purpose.
Health & fitness dataYour sex, age, height, weight, activity level and goal, plus your meals, calories and macros, workouts, water and weight logs, are stored only on your device and never uploaded to our server.
Random install ID & IP addressThe App creates a random ID when installed (not your advertising ID or device serial). It is sent with each analysis, and our server uses only a hash of it and a hash of your IP address to count daily usage and prevent abuse. These counters are kept for about 2 days.
PurchasesPro subscriptions are processed by Google Play on Android and by the Apple App Store (StoreKit 2) on iOS. To give Pro users their daily scan allowance, the App sends the store purchase credential to our server for verification: the Google Play purchase token on Android, or the StoreKit 2 transaction JWS on iOS. The server confirms with Google or Apple that the subscription is active. We never receive your card number or other payment details.
Device & crash dataNon-identifying information (device model, OS version, crash logs) may be processed through Google services solely to improve stability.
