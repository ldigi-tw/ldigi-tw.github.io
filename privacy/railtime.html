This Privacy Policy applies to “Rail Times TW” on Google Play (package tool.railtime, the “App”), developed and operated by Liberty Digital Technology Co., Ltd. (“we”, “us”). The App lets you look up timetables, fares and arrival information for Taiwan High Speed Rail, Taiwan Railway, metro systems, buses and flights. No account is required. The App is not an official app of Taiwan High Speed Rail Corporation, Taiwan Railway Corporation, any metro operator, bus company or airline.

By downloading, installing or using the App, you confirm that you have read and agree to this Policy and our Terms of Use. If you do not agree, stop using and uninstall the App. This Policy follows Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act (“PDPA”) and the Google Play Developer Program Policies.

2. Data we process

The App does not ask for your name, phone number, address or an account, and it does not access your location. To provide its features, it processes the following:

Search criteriaWhen you search, the App sends the station codes, metro system, bus city and route code, airport code, travel date and train number over HTTPS to our server (cc.ldigi.com.tw), which fetches the data from public sources and returns it. These criteria contain nothing that identifies you.
Connection recordsTo prevent abuse, the server counts recent requests using an irreversible hash of your IP address and deletes it after about 10 minutes. It is used for nothing else.
Favourites and historyFavourite routes, favourite buses, recent searches and settings are stored only on your device and are never uploaded.
Purchase and subscription statusGoogle Play tells the App whether “Remove ads” or the “Premium” subscription is active so the matching features can be unlocked. We never receive your payment details.
